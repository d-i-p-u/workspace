সাহায্য

How do I pay?

We only take Cash on Delivery (COD). Pay the rider when your parcel arrives. The total you see at checkout, delivery included, is the exact amount you pay.

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

Inside Dhaka: 1–2 working days, ৳70. Outside Dhaka: 2–4 working days, ৳130. Delivery is free on orders over ৳2,500.

How do I pick my belt size?

Take your jeans waist size and use that. S = 28–30", M = 32–34", L = 36–38", XL = 40–42". Every strap has several holes, and we can trim it when we confirm your order.

What happens after I order?

We call or message you within 24 hours to confirm. Your order then goes out with the courier. Keep your phone on!

Can I exchange?

Yes. Contact us within 7 days of delivery for a size or colour exchange, as long as the product is unused. You only pay the delivery charge.
